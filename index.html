02  Work

Built from first principles
paged-inference-engine — a mini vLLM-style inference server August 2026

A block-based paged KV cache, a continuous batching scheduler, a FlashAttention-2 Triton kernel rewritten to read and write through a block table, and INT8 KV cache quantization, all built without referencing vLLM's source. Benchmarked against a naive sequential baseline across a concurrency sweep; at this model's scale the naive baseline wins on throughput, and the writeup explains why rather than hiding it.

95→176 tok/s paged throughput, N=1 to N=32, never crosses naive's ~530-550 ~0.001 max abs diff, paged FA-2 kernel vs SDPA, scrambled block layout ~1100ms → ~69ms paged TTFT after adding kernel warmup

cpp-gpu-inference — an end-to-end GPU inference stack

CUDA and Triton kernels, FlashAttention-2 forward and backward written from scratch and wrapped in a custom torch.autograd.Function, and an LLM.int8()-style INT8 post-training quantizer — each one benchmarked against the production library it replaces (cuBLAS, PyTorch SDPA, bitsandbytes) rather than against itself. Profiled with Nsight Compute to confirm the intended mechanism actually fired at hardware level, not just that the wall clock moved.

46.4% static memory cut vs bitsandbytes' 29.3% 60.3% of FP32 peak, Triton matmul at 2048 43.13% → 3.36% L1TEX coalescing gap closed by tiling

en-de-transformer — encoder-decoder from scratch in PyTorch

6 encoder and 6 decoder blocks, 8 attention heads, d_model 512, trained on 200K Tatoeba English–German pairs. This is the substrate the FlashAttention-2 kernel and the INT8 quantizer were integrated into and measured on — a real model rather than a synthetic benchmark harness.
